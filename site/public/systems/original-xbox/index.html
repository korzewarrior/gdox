gdox / systems / original xbox

play and preserve original xbox discs through exact supported stock dvd drives.

current

gdox 0.1.3 sends a physical disc directly to xemu. it can also create a playable xiso or a full-disc preservation image.

linux, steam deck, macos, and windows builds are available. android is in development.

requirements

  • an exact supported drive profile
  • your own MCPX rom, compatible xbox bios, and game disc
  • the gdox build for your host platform

no microsoft firmware or game data is included.
