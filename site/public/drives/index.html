send a drive

if you want a particular drive supported, contact me before shipping or ordering anything.

you can loan a drive for testing and get it back, or donate it so it stays available for future updates.

include the manufacturer, full model, firmware revision, connection or usb enclosure, a clear photo of the label, and whether it is a loan or donation.
