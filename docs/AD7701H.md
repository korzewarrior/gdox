# HP AD-7701H 2.85

Experimental original Xbox disc reader for Windows USB/SPTI:

- Inquiry: `hp DVD RW AD-7701H 2.85`
- USB bridge: `13fd:0840`
- Main firmware SHA-256: `c3637cfbdc5f2f69f89a35dcddae679996e8f4fb80ab0c361cb3c64288230769`

The reader verifies the complete main firmware, normal operating mode, stock
capacity and XGD1 physical layout before changing two volatile geometry bytes.
It reads up to 16 sectors per command. Closing restores both bytes, checks their
values, and verifies the original capacity and ordinary DVD read. Failed
restoration retains ownership so cleanup can be retried. Unknown RAM states are
not overwritten. The adapter does not flash firmware, enter service mode, reset
the drive, or operate its tray.

On October 22, 2026, the compiled C adapter read Unreal Championship's XDVDFS
descriptor, directory, and complete 4,288,512-byte `default.xbe` through the
diagnostic connection. The executable identifies title `49470024`. Samples on
both layers and across the layer boundary matched independent single-sector
reads. Restoration and ordinary DVD reads passed afterward.

Native application discovery, sustained gameplay and a complete disc image have
not been validated on this drive. Security-sector evidence is not exposed by
this adapter. Other firmware images, bridges, host transports and Xbox 360 discs
are not covered. Disconnecting the drive during activation can require a
physical reconnect; restoration cannot be guaranteed after transport loss.
